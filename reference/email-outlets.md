# Email outlets

Six outlets that take event listings by email. One message each covers a whole cycle, so
this is the best reach-per-minute of any channel — roughly fifteen minutes for all six.

Draft in **Outlook on the web under matt@trlibrary.com**. Include a friendly note with genuine
gratitude; these are small newsrooms doing the Library a favor by running free listings.

| Outlet | Address | Reach | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **North Dakota Living** | ndliving@ndarec.com | ~200K households statewide | **Deadline: the 10th of the prior month.** Statewide electric co-op magazine — the single biggest email-channel reach. |
| **InForum / The Forum (Fargo)** | calendar@forumcomm.com | Fargo metro | ⚠️ Address came from a search result, not the paper's own page — **verify before sending.** |
| **Glendive Chamber (MT)** | glendive.events@gmail.com | Eastern Montana | Wants at least one week's notice. |
| **KFYR-TV** (Gray, Bismarck) | station contact | Bismarck | Tockify calendar has no public form — email is the only route. |
| **KQCD** (Gray, Dickinson) | station contact | Dickinson | Closest TV market to Medora. |
| **KUMV** (Gray, Williston) | station contact | Williston | |

## Geography matching

Don't send everything to everyone. Match the outlet to the event:

- **Medora events** → all six
- **Bismarck events** → ND Living, InForum, KFYR; skip Glendive and Dickinson/Williston stations
- **Marquee events only** → pitch a story angle to KFYR and KX assignment desks separately;
  a calendar listing and a segment pitch are different asks

## What a good message looks like

Short. One paragraph of warmth, a clean block of listings, a link, and an offer to supply
art or an interview.

> Subject: Theodore Roosevelt Presidential Library — October events for your calendar
>
> Hi [name],
>
> Thank you for continuing to run community events — it genuinely helps us reach people
> across the state who'd otherwise never hear about these.
>
> Here's what's coming up at the Library in Medora this month:
>
> **[Event]** — [Day, date], [time] MT
> [One sentence of what it is.] [Free / $X.]
> [link to the event's own page]
>
> [repeat]
>
> Happy to send photos or arrange an interview with anyone on our team if that's useful.
>
> Matt Briney
> Chief Communications & Marketing Officer
> Theodore Roosevelt Presidential Library
> hello@trlibrary.com · 701-892-6070
